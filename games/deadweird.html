🎯 Goal

Survive zombie waves as long as possible. Kill zombies to earn points. Chain kills for COMBO multipliers. Collect power-ups. Clear a wave and a harder one begins.

💡 Pro Tips

Keep moving — standing still gets you surrounded. Watch your ammo — hit R before it hits zero. Grab Hearts ❤️ when HP drops. Combos multiply your score — never break your streak!
